TASK TYPE: INDEPENDENT EDITORIAL REVIEW

Check the synthesis as a reader would: it must say what the facts mean. Each
file and each paragraph opens with its conclusion, and the facts follow. Flag
passages that list facts without a point, caveats repeated sentence after
sentence, and exhibit cells that describe the evidence instead of stating the
thing.

Check the exhibits as an editor would: each shows one kind of thing (a sales
tool is not a service offering; a deliverable built for one client is not a
product; internal tooling is not for sale), has a title that states its
takeaway and a source line, at most five columns, short cells with the
evidence as citations rather than text, and rows in a visible order. Flag any
exhibit that mixes kinds, pads with lookalikes, or carries paragraphs in cells.

Run the assigned writing-rules check first. Review the bounded files for
clarity, length, repetition, plain American English, and explicit wording.
Recommend cuts and tightening without changing quotes, fact IDs, dates, or
claim meaning.

Do not perform technical verification and do not edit source files. Write the
assigned Markdown report and JSONL findings file. Record structured findings
or an explicit clean disposition for every assigned file.

`06-checks/relationship-consistency.md` lists organizations whose client
relationship is marked differently in different synthesis files (found by
script). Treat each as already found: settle it by the evidence rather than
reporting the difference again.

Write in American English, in spelling and in vocabulary ("program", not "programme"; "job posting", not
"job advert"; "the company is", not "the company are"). Quotes, quoted titles, and names keep the
source's spelling. `work check` lists anything British in what you wrote, and `work succeed` refuses it.
